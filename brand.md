# CargoLens visual identity

CargoLens is a precise, calm workspace for shipping operations teams. Evidence and next actions carry the visual hierarchy. The premium business character comes from confident typography, generous alignment and a restrained blue system.

## Mark and artwork

Use a geometric lens surrounding a pair of document lines. Keep the wordmark in a medium-weight sans face. Borrow the compact silhouette of Dropbox, the clear wordmark of Linear and the utilitarian legibility of IBM; do not copy their marks. Custom document-and-lens SVG art introduces the workflow on the login and empty inbox surfaces.

## Palette

Reference: [Talentbank](https://www.talentbank.ai/), rendered styles inspected on 21 September 2026. Adapt the colours and type to an operational workspace without copying the site's content or marks.

- Navy #16284b: primary ink and actions; deep navy #0a2542 for navigation.
- Ivory canvas #f7f6f2; surface #fffefa; muted ink #52647d.
- Soft gold #e7ce93: mark and decorative accents on navy, never small text on ivory.
- Green #246345, amber #865118 and red #963c3c: semantic outcomes, always paired with text.

## Type and density

Albert Sans is the interface family. Fraunces gives page-level and sign-in headings the reference site's editorial character; evidence tables, controls and badges remain sans serif. Both fonts are bundled locally under the SIL Open Font License in `packages/brand/fonts`, without third-party font requests. Extension inbox fonts use a namespaced family so the host mailbox is unaffected, with system fallbacks if font loading is blocked.

Use a 4px spacing grid, 8px control corners, 12px major surface corners and tabular numerals. Body text is 14px; headings range from 20px to 32px. Interface copy uses direct, professional language.

## Imagery and icons

Use original geometric SVG artwork, no stock imagery. Lucide line icons are the single icon family. Decorative art stays out of dense evidence tables. UI motion is limited to short opacity changes, with reduced-motion support.

## Principles and touchpoints

1. Evidence before certainty: pair status labels with source details and explicit blockers.
2. One visual action: emphasize the next supported operation, never a fabricated approval.
3. Quiet precision: use spacing and separators rather than decorative cards.
4. Honest measurement: distinguish live processing, cached results and event replay.

The dashboard and extension share the navy and ivory palette, locally bundled type and status language. Documentation and presentation graphics use the mark, blue rules and real evidence; social graphics may use the document illustration without invented performance claims.
